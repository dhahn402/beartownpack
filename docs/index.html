How it works

If you look after animals in Craven County — sitting, walking, grooming, training, boarding, rescue, fostering — this is the whole arrangement.

1
You own your domain It is registered in your name. If you ever leave, you take your site and your domain with you, free, without having to ask.
2
We build and look after the site A real website, your Google listing kept accurate, small edits when your hours or prices change, and a plain monthly note about who found you.
3
It costs almost nothing while you are starting Fifty dollars a month under $1,500 of monthly revenue. It rises only as you earn, and it drops again the month you do not.
4
Never a cut of your business No ownership, no percentage of your bookings, no holding your money. We are a supplier, not a partner.
